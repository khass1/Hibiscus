No plant runs every format with the same efficiency, and none should. Sticks, pressed powder and multifunctional products with sun protection each demand their own process, equipment and validation — an investment that rarely pays for itself on a single portfolio item.

That is where we come in. A large share of what we produce is for other cosmetics manufacturers: companies that already manufacture, already know the process, and need a capability they do not have in house — or extra capacity at a specific moment. **The product ships under your brand, to your customers, with the documentation your quality control requires.**

This page answers what matters to someone already running a plant: what we produce, what we guarantee contractually, and how regulatory responsibility is divided between the parties.

## Where you are

**A customer asked for a format you do not produce.** Turning the order down means opening the door to another supplier on that account. We produce the item under your brand and the customer stays yours.

**The line is full.** A seasonal peak, a filling bottleneck, or a line tied up by another product — additional capacity without hiring or buying equipment.

**The formulation is yours, but filling is the bottleneck.** You bring the finished bulk and we fill, label and finish it.

**You want to test a category before investing.** A single batch in a new format lets you measure market response before tying up capital in machinery and validation.

**The product requires a substantiated claim.** SPF, for instance, depends on a tested base and on documentation that supports the claim. We already hold a base and SPF 50 studies for stick and BB cream.

## What we run

**Multifunctional sun protection with SPF 50 studies.** We maintain our own **stick** and **BB cream** bases with completed SPF 50 studies. For anyone entering the category without developing and testing a base from scratch, it is the fastest available starting point — how far the studies apply to the finished product depends on the degree of customisation and on the project's regulatory classification.

**Formats with per-unit minimums.** Stick from 500 units and pressed powder from 800 units — volumes that make entering a new format viable without committing to scale.

**Facial, body, hair care and make-up ranges**, in cream, gel, serum, oil, lotion, soap, balm, scrub, stick and pressed powder. The [capability matrix](/en/what-we-do/#capacidades) shows the full format-by-range combination.

## Confidentiality and commercial protection

This is the question a manufacturer asks before any other, and it is not the question a brand asks. It is not only about protecting a product idea — it is about knowing whether the production partner can become a commercial problem.

Our commitment, put in writing before any technical detail is exchanged:

- **We do not approach your customer base.** The channels and customers you serve do not become ours because we know your product.
- **Your formulation is not supplied to anyone else.** What you bring us, or what we develop specifically for you, is confidential and is not produced for another client. Technical documentation is retained solely for batch traceability and regulatory obligation.
- **The relationship is not publicised.** We do not name clients, do not use logos and do not publish identifiable cases without written authorisation.

**Your own formula.** We manufacture formulations you already hold. We adapt them to our process, validate them in a pilot batch and produce. The formulation remains yours: it does not enter our library of bases, nor does it serve as a starting point for another project.

## Compliance and documentation

We hold full licences across every regulatory body relevant to cosmetics manufacturing in Brazil — **Anvisa, IBAMA, CETESB, the Civil Police, the Federal Police and the Army (DFPC)** — operating under Good Manufacturing Practices.

In practice, contracting a licensed, documented, audit-open plant reduces the operational risk of outsourcing. The technical and regulatory responsibilities of each party are still defined by the product, the ownership arrangement and the contract.

Every batch ships with the documentation your quality control needs to release the product and to answer an audit:

<li><strong>Test report and certificate of analysis (CoA)</strong> — per batch produced</li>

## Regulatory ownership

Product registration can be held by your company or by Hibiscus, depending on how the project is structured. Each arrangement carries its own implications for technical responsibility, for how easily you can change supplier, and for the cost of fees.

For an industrial client that already holds its own operating authorisation and registrations, keeping ownership is the most common route — the product enters your portfolio and stays yours, regardless of who manufactures it. We set out the paths in [Anvisa registration](/en/what-we-do/#registro).

## Volumes and lead times

Minimum order of **20 kg per SKU** for products sold by weight; **500 units** for stick and **800 units** for pressed powder. Up to **30 days** from briefing to first sample and **10 working days** of production, counted from the arrival of raw materials.

For technology transfer with a formula already defined, development is shorter — the work concentrates on process adaptation and pilot batch validation.

## How it starts

A signed NDA before any technical information changes hands. Then the scope: format, estimated volume, whether the formula comes from you or from us, and whether the service is full or bulk only. With that we can answer on feasibility and lead time before either side invests.
